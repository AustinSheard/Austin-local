🌊 Surf season notes
  • Southwest coast (Midigama, Ahangama, Hiriketiya): season starts late Oct and runs to April.
  • East coast (Arugam Bay) is May–Sept, so it's winding down.
  • North doesn't fit the beaches-and-mountains brief.
  • Hiriketiya bay is the friendliest wave for Shari; Midigama reefs (Lazy Left) for Austin, go early.
  • Nightlife is mellow, peaks Nov–Apr.
🚕 Getting around
  • Private driver or taxi between regions, then tuk-tuks or scooters locally.
  • Skip buses (they don't slow down, dangerous).
  • Check licence and insurance rules before riding a scooter.
