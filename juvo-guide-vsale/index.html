Product guide for machine buyers and operators

A self-cleaning smoothie vending system, for operators.

The two-unit system is sold as an upfront capital purchase. Customers tap to pay at the freezer and take a cup of frozen ingredients. The blender adds liquid, blends it in the cup and then cleans itself.

The buyer owns the machine and operates it: curating the product range, restocking, cleaning and food safety compliance. Juvo provides the machine technology, the machine software under an ongoing licence, on-call support and replacement parts under warranty.

Common questions
What do I buy?

The freezer and blender, as an upfront capital purchase. You own the machine. The machine software is provided under an ongoing licence.

What do I pay on an ongoing basis?

The software licence fee. Your other running costs are your own: product, any host-site commission or rent, electricity, labour and travel, and insurance.

Can the machine carry my branding?

Yes. The machine technology is white-labelled.

Who monitors the machine?

You do. The machine software shows status, stock and faults, and sends alerts to you. Juvo is on call if you need support.

Who makes the product?

You curate your own flavours and manufacturing processes, and source the cups.

Who handles food safety compliance?

You manage food safety compliance for your product.

What happens if a part fails?

The machine software flags the fault. Juvo is on call to support you and supplies replacement parts under the warranty arrangement.

    04Customer flow

    From purchase to drink

    What a customer does at the freezer and the blender. Payment is by card or phone on the payment terminal. No staff are needed at any step.

    STEP 1 OF 5
    1. Freezer 1

      Tap to pay

      The customer taps a card or phone on the payment terminal beside the door. This places a temporary hold. The customer is charged only for what they take.

    2. Freezer 2

      Open the door and take a cup

      The door unlocks with a click and a green light. The customer takes any cup. The freezer registers what is taken and charges only for that.

      About 30 seconds at the freezer.

    3. Blender 3

      Scan the cup on the blender

      The customer holds the QR code on the lid under the scanner. The blender reads which product it is and sets the blend.

    4. Blender 4

      Lid off, cup in

      The customer peels off the lid and places the cup in the empty blending chamber.

    5. Blender 5

      Press start and collect

      The blender adds liquid and blends in the cup, in about 60 seconds. The customer takes the smoothie. The blender then rinses itself at 90°C with a cleaning solution, in about 30 seconds.

    The team behind the machine

    1

    The business we built

    We were fed up with over-processed, sugary and expensive drinks being the only thing on offer after training. Powders were easy to forget and hard to clean. Making a proper smoothie at home took time, and the habit never lasted. Real, wholefood nutrition just wasn't there when we needed it.

    Juvo was founded in Perth by Josh, Kese and Laine. We operate our own machines in market, across Perth and Sydney, at sites that include a gym and a corporate office. The team is ten people.

    2

    What operating real machines taught us

    Running machines at real sites showed where the work is: managing reliability and ensuring a consistently great product.

    3

    Why we built our own machine

    We wanted reliable machines that deliver a great product, every time. We spent months developing our own hardware and software around those two goals. That work is the system in this guide.
